Bio

I am Yi Cade, an undergraduate student in Computer Science and Technology at Hunan University.

My current interests lie in multimodal learning, human-centered AI, affective and cognitive computing, foundation models, and AI agents. I am particularly curious about how AI systems can combine information from text, audio, vision, and context to better understand human emotion, cognition, state, and intent.

I am especially interested in AI systems that are not only capable of recognizing patterns, but can also integrate different sources of information, generalize to new situations, reason about complex inputs, and ultimately become genuinely useful to people.

I am currently strengthening my foundations in machine learning and deep learning, including neural networks, model training and optimization, CNNs, self-attention and Transformers, representation learning, pretraining, and generative AI. I enjoy learning through implementation, experiments, and asking why a model succeeds or fails.

My previous MiniWorld3D project gave me hands-on experience with running pretrained models, designing controlled experiments, evaluating results quantitatively, and organizing a complete AI project. Through this process, I found that I particularly enjoy model- and experiment-driven research and hope to gradually develop the ability to form and test my own research ideas.

Projects

MiniWorld3D

Multi-view 3D Reconstruction and Camera Pose Evaluation with VGGT

A mini research project exploring multi-view 3D reconstruction and camera geometry with VGGT.

I built a controllable synthetic 3D scene in Blender and generated 24 multi-view RGB images with ground-truth camera poses. I evaluated VGGT-1B under 3, 6, 12, and 24-view settings and aligned predicted camera trajectories with Blender ground truth using Sim(3) alignment.

In the current synthetic experiment, the 24-view setting achieved an ATE RMSE of 0.0459, approximately 36.7% lower than the 6-view setting.
